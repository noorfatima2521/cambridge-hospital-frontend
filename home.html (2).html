COMING TO HOSPITAL

The hospital is open 24/7 for patient care. Emergency Services, surgeries, cancer treatments and other acute care and diagnostic services are offered at Cambridge Memorial Hospital.

PATIENT REGISTRATION

The patient registration process is an important first step to receiving an exceptional care experience at our hospital.

PATIENT RELATIONS

At Cambridge Memorial Hospital, we take pride in providing exceptional patient care. Should you have a question, concern or compliment regarding your experience, we would be happy to address this with you.

DISCHARGE

You will be discharged when you no longer need to receive inpatient care and can safely go home. Or, you may be discharged to another type of facility that can provide the level of care you need.

Description

Cambridge General Hospital is a trusted healthcare institution dedicated to providing high-quality medical services to the local community and beyond. With a strong focus on patient care, safety, and innovation, the hospital combines advanced medical technology with compassionate, human-centered treatment.Serving a wide range of specialties, Cambridge General Hospital offers comprehensive services including outpatient care, emergency treatment, specialist consultations, and surgical procedures.
