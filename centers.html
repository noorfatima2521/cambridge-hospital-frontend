EXPLORE OUR CENTERS OF EXCELLENCE




Explore our Centers of Excellence to learn how specialized expertise, modern facilities, and patient-centered care come together to support your health and well-being.



CANCER CENTER

Cambridge Hospital, part of Cambridge Health Alliance, offers a cancer care program where specialists in hematology and oncology provide diagnosis and treatment for many types of cancer.

HEART & VASCULAR CENTER

The Heart & Vascular services include a full range of cardiac testing such as echocardiograms, EKGs, stress tests, Holter monitoring, and vascular exams to assess heart function and detect cardiovascular issues.

MATERNITY CENTER

The unit includes a dedicated Labor & Delivery area and Postpartum Unit, helping many growing families each year with personalized and culturally sensitive care environment for low-risk pregnancies..


NEUROLOGYCENTER

The Neurology Center at Cambridge Hospital provides expert care for disorders of the brain, spinal cord, nerves, and muscles, offering diagnosis, treatment, and ongoing management for a wide range of neurological conditions.


PEDIATRICS CENTER

CHA’s pediatric services are recognized for strong patient-provider communication and family-centered care, and include primary care and coordination with specialty services when needed.integrating clinical excellence with community .

EMERGENCY CENTER

It offers prompt assessment and treatment for a wide range of emergencies — including severe pain, injuries, chest pain, breathing difficulties, and other critical health issues — with on-site diagnostics and support services.
